Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe. Your payment information is securely processed and never stored on our servers.

What's the difference between model tiers?

Budget tier uses faster, cost-effective models. Premium tier uses the latest flagship models like GPT-4 Turbo and Claude 3 Opus for better reasoning.

Do unused debates roll over?

No, free tier debates reset daily at midnight UTC. Pro users have unlimited debates so rollover isn't needed.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer refunds within 7 days of your first payment if you're not satisfied. Contact support for assistance.

Do you offer team or enterprise plans?

Not yet, but we're working on it! Contact us if you're interested in team pricing.
