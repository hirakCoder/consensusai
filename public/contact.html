Frequently Asked Questions

How does ConsensusAI work?

We send your question to 4 leading AI models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Grok) and synthesize their responses to find consensus or highlight where they disagree.

Is my data private?

Yes. We don't store your questions long-term. Each query is processed and then discarded. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What's included in the Free plan?

Free users get 3 debates per day using our budget AI tier. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited debates and premium models.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.
